Fleeting Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

English (United States)

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Fleeting (“the App”) values your privacy and is designed to protect your data.

1. Data Collection

Fleeting does NOT collect or transmit:

  • Vault photos, videos, or other media files to servers operated by Fleeting
  • User account information
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data

Vault media is stored in the App's local container. Fleeting does not operate an account service or a media-upload server.

2. Photo Library Access

Fleeting requests access to your photo library for:

  • Selecting and saving photos and videos
  • Detecting recent media through Quick Import
  • Organizing media and scheduling deletion

Selected media is processed locally and is not uploaded to a Fleeting server.

3. Data Storage

  • Vault media and related metadata are stored locally on your device.
  • Fleeting has no built-in cloud sync. Depending on your Apple device-backup settings, app data may be included in a device or iCloud backup managed by Apple.
  • When an item expires, it moves to Recently Deleted. It is permanently purged later; iOS background scheduling may delay cleanup.

Fleeting for Mac

Fleeting for Mac imports images, videos, and folders chosen through the macOS file picker, drag and drop, Quick Drop, the clipboard, Shortcuts, a connected Screenshots folder, or a user-configured Watched Folder. Supported media is copied into Fleeting's sandboxed app container on this Mac. Standard imports leave the original files unchanged. If the user explicitly enables source cleanup for Screenshots or Watched Folders, Fleeting moves imported originals to the macOS Trash only after the vault copy and manifest commit succeed.

Vault media and its metadata remain in the local app container and are not uploaded to a Fleeting server. The Mac app does not request access to the Photos library or Camera. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

When Automatic Cleanup is enabled, an expired item moves to Fleeting's Trash. It remains restorable until the user permanently deletes it or Fleeting removes it after the configured Trash retention period. Export copies only the items the user selects to a folder the user chooses.

Opening Privacy Policy or Support connects the user's default browser to this GitHub Pages website. Depending on the user's Mac backup configuration, local app data may be included in Time Machine or another backup the user configures.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Fleeting does not send vault media to advertising, analytics, or Fleeting-operated servers. If you share a direct HTTP(S) media URL with the Share Extension, the App contacts that URL's host to inspect and download the media you requested. Opening Privacy or Support contacts the GitHub Pages website.

5. Advertising & Tracking

Fleeting does not use:

  • Ads
  • Tracking
  • Analytics SDKs

6. Permissions

Fleeting may request:

  • Photo Library access: for selecting, saving, and organizing photos and videos
  • Camera access: for capturing temporary photos

Fleeting may use Face ID or device biometric authentication to unlock the app.
Biometric data is processed only on the device and is never collected or stored by Fleeting.

Permissions can be changed anytime in device Settings.

7. User Control

Users can:

  • Revoke permissions
  • Delete data
  • Remove the app

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in the app or website.

9. Contact

Email: ciisil@icloud.com

Fleeting is built to keep vault media private and locally managed while clearly identifying user-directed network requests and Apple-managed backups.
